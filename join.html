Ph.D. Opportunities at NJIT

The Intelligent Transportation Lab (INTR Lab) at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT) is actively seeking highly motivated Ph.D. students to join our cutting-edge research team. Our lab collaborates with leading institutions, government agencies, and industry partners to develop innovative AI-driven solutions for intelligent transportation systems.

Research Areas

  • 🚆 Digital Twins & Intelligent Operations: AI-driven rail and metro systems, predictive maintenance, multi-source sensor data fusion
  • 🚗 Smart Mobility & Traffic Optimization: AI-enhanced traffic systems, autonomous vehicle infrastructure, shared mobility solutions
  • 🛣️ Infrastructure Management: Resilient infrastructure design, predictive analytics, network optimization

Position Requirements

  • Strong academic background in Transportation Engineering, Data Science, Computer Science, Mathematics, or related fields.
  • Proficiency in programming (Python, C++, MATLAB, R) and familiarity with AI/ML models or optimization techniques.
  • Strong research potential demonstrated through previous academic work, projects, or publications.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English (TOEFL/IELTS scores may be required).
  • High motivation, teamwork abilities, and a passion for solving real-world transportation challenges.

Funding & Benefits

  • 💰 Full scholarship covering tuition and living stipend
  • 🔬 Access to state-of-the-art research facilities and high-performance computing clusters
  • 🌍 Collaboration with top-tier universities and industry leaders worldwide
  • 🎓 Opportunities for conference presentations, industry internships, and global exchange programs

How to Apply

Interested candidates should send their CV, transcripts, and a brief research statement to yun.bai@njit.edu.
