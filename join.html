Ph.D. Opportunities at NJIT

The Intelligent Transportation Lab (INTR Lab) at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT) is seeking highly motivated Ph.D. students to join our research team. Our lab collaborates with top institutions, government agencies, and industry partners to develop cutting-edge solutions for transportation challenges.

🔍 Research Areas

  • 🛣️ Transportation Infrastructure Management
  • 🚗 Multimodal Smart Mobility & Freight Logistics
  • 🚆 AI-based Tansportation Data Analytics

📌 Position Requirements

  • Strong background in Transportation Engineering, Data Science, Computer Science, or related fields
  • Proficiency in Python, C++, MATLAB, or R, and familiarity with AI/ML techniques
  • Demonstrated research potential through academic projects, publications, or previous experience
  • Excellent English communication skills (TOEFL/IELTS scores may be required)
  • Motivated individuals with teamwork abilities and a passion for transportation solutions

💰 Funding & Benefits

  • Full scholarship covering tuition and living stipend
  • Access to state-of-the-art research facilities and computing clusters
  • Collaboration with top universities and industry leaders worldwide
  • Opportunities for conference presentations, industry internships, and global exchange

📩 How to Apply

Interested candidates should send their CV, transcripts, and a brief research statement to yun.bai@njit.edu.
