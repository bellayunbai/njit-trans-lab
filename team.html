Meet Our Team

Faculty

Prof. Yun Bai

Assistant Professor, NJIT

Prof. Bai is an Assistant Professor in Intelligent Transportation at NJIT. She was previously an Assistant Research Professor at Rutgers University and holds a Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from UIUC. Her research focuses on data analytics, mathematical modeling, and transportation systems optimization. She has led multiple projects funded by USDA, USDOT, NJDOT, NJ Transit, and published over 20 papers. She serves as an invited member of the TRB AT030 Committee.
